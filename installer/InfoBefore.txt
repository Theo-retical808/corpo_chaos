ABOUT THE GAME

Corporate Chaos is an offline, single-player business simulation where you take the helm of a company and steer it through 30 years of corporate life — one quarter at a time.

Hire employees, manage six departments, make executive decisions, survive random crises, and try to build a billion-dollar empire or capture 65% market share before your 120 quarters run out.

Features:
  • Story Mode — Learn the ropes with Secretary Joan guiding you through a narrative-driven tutorial with branching dialogue and character relationships
  • Sandbox Mode — Jump straight in with all features unlocked, in a 120-quarter challenge or endless free play
  • Dynamic Chaos Engine — Random events driven by your company stats keep every playthrough unpredictable
  • Employee Management — Hire, assign, transfer, and fire employees across Marketing, Operations, Finance, HR, IT, and Research
  • Executive Decisions — Marketing campaigns, R&D investments, company retreats, crisis consultants, business loans, and cost cutting
  • Detailed Financials — Quarterly revenue/expense breakdowns, financial reports, and performance tracking
  • Fully Customizable — Edit JSON data files to change employee names, events, game balance, and job descriptions without any coding

AI-GENERATED CONTENT DISCLOSURE

All game mechanics, features, systems, and design decisions were conceived and defined by the developer. Generative AI was used as a development tool to bring these ideas to life — writing the code, producing assets, and drafting text based on the developer's specifications and direction.

AI-assisted content includes but is not limited to:

  • Source code and game logic (implemented from developer-defined designs)
  • UI layouts and visual styling (built to developer specifications)
  • Story dialogue, event descriptions, and narrative text
  • Employee names and job descriptions
  • Documentation and installer text
  • Character portrait images

The creative vision, game design, feature set, balancing decisions, and architectural choices are the developer's own work. Generative AI served as the implementation tool, not the designer.

All AI-generated output has been reviewed and curated by the developer. However, AI-generated material may contain inaccuracies, unintended patterns, or artifacts. The game is provided as-is for entertainment purposes.
