# Corporate Chaos - Product Overview

Corporate Chaos is a turn-based business management simulation game built in WPF/C#. The game gamifies the experience of running a company through strategic decision-making across multiple quarters.

## Core Concept
Players manage a company over 30 years (120 quarters) with the goal of reaching 65% market share or building a billion-dollar empire. The game features two main modes:

- **Story Mode**: Guided tutorial experience with Secretary Joan providing step-by-step learning over 8 quarters. Currently being expanded into a comprehensive 120-quarter narrative experience with character development, meaningful choices, and multiple story paths.
- **Sandbox Mode**: Full access to all features from the start, with Corporate Challenge (120 quarters) or Endless Mode options

## Key Features
- **Turn-Based Quarterly System**: Each decision is processed quarterly with financial and operational impacts
- **Employee Management**: Hire, assign, and manage employees across 6 departments (Marketing, Operations, Finance, HR, IT, Research)
- **Dynamic Chaos Engine**: Random events influenced by company stats (reputation, morale, risk) that create unpredictable challenges
- **Executive Decisions**: Strategic choices like marketing campaigns, company retreats, crisis management
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Events and challenges scale based on quarter number and company performance
- **Comprehensive Analytics**: Detailed quarterly summaries and performance tracking

## Target Experience
The game balances entertainment with business education, providing insights into corporate management while maintaining engaging gameplay through the chaos event system and strategic decision-making.
